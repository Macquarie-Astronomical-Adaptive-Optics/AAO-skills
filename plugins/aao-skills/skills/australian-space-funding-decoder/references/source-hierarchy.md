# Australian space-funding source hierarchy

Use current official sources and record the access date.

## Operational sources

1. `GrantConnect exact Grant Opportunity`
   - https://www.grants.gov.au/
   - Use for the official Grant Opportunity number, current status, dates, Guidelines, addenda, questions/answers, and attachments.
2. `Exact Grant Opportunity Guidelines`
   - Download from GrantConnect or the administering department's official page.
   - Use for legal eligibility, merit criteria, grant amount/rate, contributions, eligible expenditure, assessment, agreement, reporting, and definitions.
3. `Australian Space Agency funding hub`
   - https://www.space.gov.au/grant-programs-and-support-opportunities
   - Use to identify current and adjacent national programs relevant to the space sector.
4. `business.gov.au Grants and Programs Finder and exact program page`
   - https://business.gov.au/grants-and-programs
   - Use for discovery, application links, program summaries, and official guidance, while retaining GrantConnect/Guidelines as authority when applicable.
5. `Australian Research Council`
   - https://www.arc.gov.au/funding-research
   - Use the exact scheme page, current Grant Guidelines, Instructions to Applicants, key dates, and Research Management System material for ARC routes.
6. `Administering entity and submission portal`
   - Follow the exact Guidelines to the named department, portal, help desk, and authorised submission method.

## Source cautions

- The Space Agency page deliberately routes to programs run by several agencies; it is not a single open-call catalogue.
- GrantConnect access can require interactive browsing and may block generic crawlers. Record the exact notice and downloaded documents.
- A business.gov.au summary does not override the Guidelines.
- Program names can persist across rounds while eligibility, dates, amounts, priorities, and forms change.
- An announcement of awarded funding or a previous round is not an open opportunity.
- ARC and Commonwealth industry grants use different definitions, portals, institutional approvals, and budget rules.

# ESA Space Funding Decoder evaluation cases

## 1. Open idea versus funded implementation

**Prompt:** OSIP says anyone can submit an idea, so our Australian university is eligible for ESA funding.

**Expected behaviour:**

- Separates platform registration/idea submission from funded implementation.
- Checks the exact Campaign/Channel special conditions and applicable country rule.
- Identifies a feasible prime, subcontract, partner, host, or no-current-route conclusion from sources.

## 2. University role varies

**Prompt:** Universities can always lead ESA research activities.

**Expected behaviour:**

- Checks the exact route; does not generalise between co-sponsored research and commercial/co-funded channels.
- Verifies prime, subcontractor, co-funding, and programme-subscription conditions.
- States the implementation stage to which the rule applies.

## 3. OSIP to esa-star transition

**Prompt:** Our idea passed the OSIP stage; use the same conditions for the full proposal.

**Expected behaviour:**

- Opens the invited proposal or esa-star data pack and current special conditions.
- Rechecks entity, country, role, funding, dates, forms, and declarations.
- Does not treat idea selection as contract award or submission readiness.

## 4. ESA tender or grant

**Prompt:** Find ESA grants for supplying this flight component.

**Expected behaviour:**

- Determines whether the likely route is an ESA procurement contract rather than a grant.
- Searches esa-star Publication and labels procurement rules clearly.
- Keeps tender pricing and commercially sensitive material out of the public repository.

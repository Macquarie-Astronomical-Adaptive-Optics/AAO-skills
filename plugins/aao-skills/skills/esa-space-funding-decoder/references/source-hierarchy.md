# ESA space-funding source hierarchy

Use current official ESA sources and record the access date.

## Operational sources

1. `Exact OSIP Campaign or Channel page`
   - https://ideas.esa.int/
   - Use for live status, stages, dates, scope, eligibility, evaluation criteria, special conditions, and attached documents.
2. `OSIP overview and implementation explanation`
   - https://www.esa.int/Enabling_Support/Preparing_for_the_Future/Discovery_and_Preparation/The_Open_Space_Innovation_Platform_OSIP
   - Use for platform concepts and the routes from idea to study, co-funded research, early technology development, or other implementation.
3. `OSIP conditions`
   - Obtain the current platform terms and General Conditions of Participation from the live OSIP page.
   - Use the special conditions for the exact Campaign/Channel; they can be narrower than general platform access.
4. `esa-star Publication`
   - https://esastar-publication-ext.sso.esa.int/
   - Use for ESA tender notices, intended invitations to tender, identifiers, documents, and publication status.
5. `esa-star Tendering and entity registration`
   - Follow the current links from ESA's Doing Business with ESA and the exact tender publication.
   - Use for entity code, tender access, data packs, submission, clarification, and authorised roles.
6. `Programme-specific official pages`
   - ESA Business Applications and Space Solutions: https://business.esa.int/funding
   - ESA technology funding overview: https://technology.esa.int/page/funding-your-ideas
   - Use only when the exact opportunity belongs to that route.

## Source cautions

- Anyone may be able to engage with an idea platform while only particular legal entities and countries can implement funded work.
- Campaign and Channel conditions differ; do not carry a country list, TRL, co-funding rate, or prime rule from one to another.
- Some programmes are limited to states that subscribe to that optional ESA programme.
- Universities may be primes in one research route and barred from prime status in another commercial or co-funded route.
- OSIP idea selection may lead to a later invited proposal or esa-star tender with new conditions.
- An esa-star publication can be procurement rather than grant funding.

## Evidence record

| Field | Required value |
| --- | --- |
| Opportunity | Exact title and identifier |
| Platform | OSIP, esa-star, Business Applications, or other |
| Stage | Idea, proposal, tender, negotiation, or contract |
| Official URL | Direct page or document |
| Version/date | Publication and update date |
| Accessed | ISO date, time, timezone |
| Locator | Page, section, special condition, or portal field |
| Supports | Exact output claim |
| Status | Current, superseded, unclear, or snapshot only |

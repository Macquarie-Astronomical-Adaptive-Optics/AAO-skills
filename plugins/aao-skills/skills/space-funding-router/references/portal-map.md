Verify current details before every substantive scan.

| Platform | Strongest fit | Typical route | First hard gate | Specialist skill |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Horizon Europe | Collaborative R&I, space technology, data, infrastructure, industry and science | EU grant topic with action type and consortium rules | Country/role/funding eligibility plus topic-specific restrictions | `horizon-europe-decoder` |
| NASA NSPIRES / ROSES | Space and Earth science, astrophysics, planetary science, technology and mission research | NASA research announcement or ROSES program element | Exact solicitation eligibility and foreign-organisation funding rule | `nasa-nspires-decoder` |

## Portal distinctions

- `Grant` and `contract` are not interchangeable. ESA and EU portals contain both funding and procurement routes.
- `Can submit an idea` does not necessarily mean `can receive funding or implement it`.
- `Can participate` does not necessarily mean `can be funded`.
- A master solicitation, work programme, or agency overview is not proof that a particular opportunity is currently open.
- A platform date may be a cycle end date, rolling-window boundary, NOI date, first-stage date, final-proposal date, batch cut-off, or local closing time. Label it exactly.

## Australian starting position

For an Australian university or research group:

- Australian Commonwealth and ARC routes are geographically natural but still scheme-specific.
- Horizon Europe may permit participation while funding or coordinating rights depend on the topic and current country status.
- NASA may permit foreign participation under solicitation-specific conditions but can apply no-exchange-of-funds or other restrictions.
- ESA idea platforms may accept broad engagement, while implementation funding and prime-contractor eligibility are often limited by ESA country and procurement rules.

Do not carry these general tendencies into a decision without checking the exact opportunity.

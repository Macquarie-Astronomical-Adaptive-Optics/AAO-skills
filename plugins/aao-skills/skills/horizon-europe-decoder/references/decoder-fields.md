# Decoder fields and interpretation guide

## Structure vocabulary

Keep these layers distinct:

| Layer | Meaning | Common error |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Programme | Horizon Europe as a whole | Treating one cluster rule as programme-wide |
| Pillar | Major programme component | Skipping the route by which a topic is funded |
| Cluster or programme part | Thematic work-programme area | Searching only one likely cluster |
| Destination | Policy and expected-impact frame | Summarising the topic without its destination-level outcomes |
| Call | Container with an identifier, dates, budget, and one or more topics | Calling it a procurement tender or using the call ID as the topic ID |
| Topic | The proposal target with specific outcomes, scope, conditions, and budget | Applying generic call information without topic checks |
| Action type | RIA, IA, CSA, or another legal/funding form | Assuming the name alone determines the current funding rate |

## Decision record

For each topic, capture the following fields. Use `not stated` rather than filling gaps from habit.

### Identity and status

- exact topic identifier and title
- call identifier and destination
- live portal status
- opening date, deadline, time zone, and stage
- date and time at which the status was checked

### Purpose and technical fit

- one-sentence plain-language purpose
- expected outcomes, each kept as a separate requirement
- scope elements and explicit exclusions
- starting and ending TRL or demonstration maturity
- required users, infrastructure, data, standards, platforms, partnerships, or coordination

### Money and instrument

- action type
- funding model and rate, including exceptions
- total topic budget
- expected EU contribution per project
- indicative number of projects
- lump-sum or actual-cost treatment
- current application and budget-template versions

### Participation and eligibility

- minimum consortium and independence requirements
- coordinator rule
- country and establishment requirements
- whether an entity may participate, receive EU funding, or must self-fund
- topic-specific country, ownership/control, security, or role restrictions
- any essential-participant, associated-partner, affiliated-entity, subcontracting, or international-cooperation route

### Proposal and evaluation

- admissibility and page limits
- evaluation criteria, thresholds, and weighting
- required Part A/Part B/annex content
- ethics, security, open-science, gender-dimension, data-management, dissemination, and exploitation requirements
- topic-specific evaluation or portfolio conditions

## Fit rubric

Use this rubric without converting it to a single arithmetic score:

| Dimension | Green | Amber | Red | Unknown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Confirmed route and funding eligibility | Plausible route needs confirmation | Topic or role excludes organisation | Current rule not checked |
| Scope | Directly addresses required outcomes and scope | Partial fit or depends on reframing | Outside scope or explicit exclusion | Capabilities or scope unclear |
| TRL | Starting evidence and target align | Small maturity gap with credible plan | Starting/target maturity incompatible | TRL evidence missing |
| Consortium | Credible role and partner coverage | Important partner gap | Mandatory role/coverage cannot be met | Partner structure unknown |
| Evidence | Relevant track record and assets are documented | Evidence exists but is incomplete | Claimed capability is unsupported | No evidence reviewed |
| Timing/effort | Workable before deadline | Tight but recoverable | Deadline or workload is infeasible | Effort not estimated |

A red hard gate controls the recommendation even if most other dimensions are green.

## Special cautions for Australian organisations and space topics

- Check the current participating-countries document and any Australian complementary-funding mechanism. Do not assume that permission to join a consortium means automatic EU funding.
- Space-topic participation can be restricted to named countries or entities satisfying ownership/control conditions. Read the exact topic eligibility section and General Annex B.
- Security-sensitive project information, commercial secrets, and EU classified information are different categories. Use the current security guidance and do not infer classification from sensitivity alone.
